Built for everyone

One Platform.
Three Audiences.

🔍
For Buyers
Find Your Card.

Browse every card from every seller at the show — before you even leave home. Filter by player, sport, grade, or price.

  • Search all sellers in one place
  • Table numbers so you know exactly where to go
  • Filter by PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC or Raw
  • Message sellers via WhatsApp or Instagram DM
  • Scan a QR at the entrance to go live
📦
For Sellers
Upload Once.

Drop your existing spreadsheet. CardShow maps your columns automatically, whether you use Card Dealer Pro, Flipwise, Ludex, or Excel.

  • Smart CSV/XLSX column mapper — upload your existing spreadsheet
  • Add individual cards with smart autocomplete search
  • Seller profile with WhatsApp & Instagram contact info
  • Mark cards sold in real time from your table
  • Unique QR code — buyers contact you directly at the show
🎪
For Organizers
Run Your Show.

A dedicated Shows dashboard gives organizers full control — authorize sellers, assign tables, publish inventory, and share a public show page on Instagram in one click.

  • Shows dashboard with one-click publish per show
  • Assign table numbers to every authorized seller
  • Shareable public show page — no login needed to view
  • Generate marketing + signage QR codes
  • Separate inventory tab for full admin oversight

For Organizers

Shows First.
Always.

The admin portal leads with a dedicated Shows dashboard — not an inventory table. Every show card gives organizers full control in one place: authorize sellers, assign tables, publish inventory, and share the show page.

  • Shows tab is the default landing screen for admins — inventory is a secondary tab
  • Each show card shows card count, seller count, and tables assigned at a glance
  • ⚡ Publish Inventory pushes all cards from authorized sellers instantly
  • Setup completeness signal — see which sellers still need a table number
  • Auto-number tables 1–N in one tap for large shows

Admins switch to the Inventory tab for full catalog management across all sellers.
