Built for everyone

One Platform.
Three Audiences.

🔍
For Buyers
Find Your Card.

Browse every card from every seller at the show — before you even leave home. Filter by player, sport, grade, price, or deal score.

  • Search all sellers in one place
  • Live deal scores vs. market value
  • Filter by PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC or Raw
  • Message sellers directly on WhatsApp
  • Scan a QR at the entrance to go live
📦
For Sellers
Upload Once.

Drop your existing spreadsheet. CardShow maps your columns automatically, whether you use Card Dealer Pro, Flipwise, Ludex, or Excel.

  • Smart CSV/XLSX column mapper
  • Works with all grading services
  • Mark cards sold in real time
  • Unique QR code for your table
  • Buyers contact you via WhatsApp
🎪
For Organizers
Grow Your Show.

Create shows, authorize sellers, generate buyer-access QR codes, and give attendees a reason to show up — because they can already see what's there.

  • Create and manage multiple shows
  • Control which sellers have access
  • Generate marketing + signage QR codes
  • Admin dashboard across all inventory
  • Show-wide filters and deal tracking
Simple by design

From spreadsheet to live show
in minutes.

01
📋
Sellers Upload

Drop any CSV or spreadsheet. Our smart column mapper auto-detects your format — Card Dealer Pro, Flipwise, Ludex, or custom. No reformatting required.

02
⚡
Organizer Publishes

The show admin creates the event, authorizes sellers, and publishes inventory live. One click generates QR codes for marketing and entrance signage.

Scan the QR, enter the access code, and browse every card at the show — filtered, sorted, with live deal scores. Message any seller directly on WhatsApp.
