Welcome to CardShow
You're 3 minutes away from having your inventory live at your next card show. Let's get you set up.
  • Set up your seller profile
  • Add your first cards (manual, scan, or CSV)
  • Know what to do on show day
🏷️
Your Seller Profile
Buyers tap your QR code to browse. Give them a name and a way to reach you.
📋
Before the Show
Here's what to have ready before show day:
  • Your card inventory — graded slabs, raw cards, sealed boxes
  • Prices for each card (you can update right from your phone)
  • A phone or tablet to display your QR code at the table
  • An organizer access code to join the show
📦
Adding Your Inventory
Three ways to load your cards — pick whatever fits your workflow:
📷
Scan a card Point your camera at any PSA/BGS/CGC slab or raw card. Auto-fills player, set, grade, and parallel.
📊
Upload a spreadsheet Drop a CSV or Excel file. Download our template for the correct column layout.
✏️
Add cards manually Type in each card one at a time. Great for a quick few cards before a show.
🎪
Show Day Playbook
You're at the show. Here's the drill:
📲
Share your QR Tap "My QR Code" and prop your phone up. Buyers scan to browse your table.
🤝
Mark cards sold Tap any card → "Mark Sold" → log price and payment method.
💰
Check comps Tap "Check Comps" for live market prices before you price a card.
📊
End-of-day report Report tab shows revenue, sell-through, and best performers.
🏆
You're All Set
Your profile is saved. Start adding cards whenever you're ready — scan, upload, or type them in.
  • Profile saved
  • Scanner and CSV import ready
  • Reach out via the ? menu if you need help
