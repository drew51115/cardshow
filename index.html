Built for the hobby

The show floor record the hobby never had

Beckett built the first price guide from what dealers reported selling cards for. CardShow records what dealers actually sell cards for, at shows, across the country.

Every card marked sold on CardShow generates a permanent record. Player, grade, price, show location, date. No estimates. No averages. What cards sell for at the table.

Tracked per sale
  • Player and card details
  • Grade and grading company
  • Sold price and asking price
  • Payment method
  • Show name and location
  • Table number and seller
  • Date and time
Why it matters
  • eBay shows what cards sell for online
  • CardShow shows what they sell for at shows
  • Regional price differences, visible for the first time
  • Grade premiums at shows vs online, tracked over time
  • The comp layer the hobby has been missing

Every show adds to the record. Run your show on CardShow

ShowVision

Add a Card
in 3 Seconds.

Point your camera at any PSA, BGS, or CGC slab. ShowVision reads the cert barcode automatically — or snap a photo and it identifies the player, year, set, grade, and parallel from the label alone. No typing. No searching.

  • Barcode detection reads PSA, CGC, and SGC cert numbers instantly
  • ShowVision identifies cards even without a cert number — raw cards included
  • Auto-fills player, year, set, card number, parallel, grade, and grader
  • Confidence indicators flag uncertain fields in amber or red before you save
  • Live card database — search by name to auto-fill from millions of cards
  • Works in any browser on iOS or Android — no app download required
  • Scan-to-Sell — photograph a walk-up sale that was never pre-listed and confirm it on the spot

Live Market Data

Know What Every
Card Is Worth.

CardShow pulls real completed sales from eBay — not estimates, not list prices. Sellers price with confidence before the show. Buyers see market value right in the card detail, with a badge that calls out underpriced cards on the spot.

  • Sellers: "Check Comps" prices your whole inventory in one click
  • Matches exact parallel, grade, and grader — Silver PSA 10 ≠ base PSA 10
  • Buyers see market value on every card they tap — Deal / Fair / High badge
  • Recent sale dates and prices shown — buyers know if a deal is actually a deal
  • Sports cards via CardSight AI real-sale data; Pokémon via TCGPlayer market prices
  • 24-hour cache prevents redundant lookups mid-show
