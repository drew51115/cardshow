For Sellers · ShowVision

Add a Card
in Seconds.

Snap a photo of a slab or a raw card and ShowVision fills in the player, year, set, card number, parallel, grader, and grade. Or photograph a whole case, or drop in the spreadsheet you already have.

  • One card: snap a photo and review the filled-in fields
  • Whole case: photograph a showcase and review every card it finds before saving
  • Spreadsheet: Shopify, Card Dealer Pro, Flipwise, Ludex, TCGplayer, or any CSV/Excel file. Columns map automatically
  • Type it in with a guided search: pick the player, set, card number, and parallel
  • Amber and red highlights flag anything ShowVision isn't sure about
  • Duplicate warning if a card is already in your inventory
  • Every graded card is checked against the Global Trading Card Registry. You're alerted if it's been reported lost or stolen, and it stays off the show floor until you decide
  • Works in any phone browser. No app to install

For Sellers · At the Table

Price It Right.
Sell It Fast.

Check comps on your whole inventory in one click, then record every sale at the table in a couple of taps. Your report is ready the moment the doors close.

  • Check Comps prices your inventory from real sales, matched to the exact parallel, grade, and grader
  • Comps only appear when the match is high confidence. No shaky guesses
  • One + Sell / Add button: sell a card from your inventory, or snap a walk-up card you never listed
  • Record sold price and payment method. Every sale is dated, even across multi-day shows

  • Turn any sale into a branded share card for Instagram or X
  • Your profile (display name, WhatsApp, Instagram) is shown only to buyers at your shows
  • Table QR code that opens your own storefront

Built for the hobby

The show floor record the hobby never had

Beckett built the first price guide from what dealers reported selling cards for. CardShow records what dealers actually sell cards for, at shows, across the country.

Every card marked sold on CardShow generates a permanent record. Player, grade, price, show location, date. No estimates. No averages. What cards sell for at the table.

Tracked per sale
  • Player and card details
  • Grade and grading company
  • Sold price and asking price
  • Payment method
  • Show name and location
  • Table number and seller
  • Date and time
Why it matters
  • eBay shows what cards sell for online
  • CardShow shows what they sell for at shows
  • Regional price differences, visible for the first time
  • Grade premiums at shows vs online, tracked over time
  • The comp layer the hobby has been missing

Official Partner
Certified Trading Card Association
CTCA  ·  Safer. Smarter. Stronger.  ·  Established 2025
CardShow is proud to be an official partner of the Certified Trading Card Association — the nonprofit trade association bringing card shops, collectors, manufacturers, and grading companies together to make the trading card hobby safer, more trusted, and more professional.

Integration Partner
Global Trading Card Registry
GTCR  ·  Register. Verify. Protect. Transfer.
CardShow partners with the Global Trading Card Registry, the independent trust registry for graded trading cards. Vendors can register their graded cards with the GTCR as they add them to their CardShow inventory, and every graded card is checked against the registry by cert number. If a card has been reported lost or stolen, CardShow alerts the vendor before it ever goes live to buyers.
